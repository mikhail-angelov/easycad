Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

text2part is a solo-operated tool. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the service, and this page says plainly what that is.

What we store

  • Account email. If you sign in, we store your email address to identify your account and send sign-in links. There are no passwords.
  • Settings. Your chosen provider, model, and preferences.
  • CAD sessions. Your prompts, generated code, and models are held in memory on the server for the life of the session so the editor and timeline work; they are not a long-term archive.
  • Feedback. If you submit the in-app feedback form, we store your message, optional rating, and optional email.

Your LLM key (BYOK)

If you add your own LLM provider key, it is encrypted at rest and used only to make model calls on your behalf. It is never logged and never shown back to you in full. You can remove it at any time from the account panel.

Analytics

We use Yandex.Metrica to understand aggregate usage (page visits, funnel steps). It is only active when configured for the deployment. We do not sell data or run advertising trackers.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account and its stored data at any time from the account panel in the app (Delete account). This removes your email, settings, and stored key.

Contact

Questions? Reach the operator via the in-app feedback form or the GitHub repository.
